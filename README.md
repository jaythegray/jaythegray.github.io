# Privacy Policy for Perfect Stop

**Effective Date:** September 29, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 29, 2026

Perfect Stop is a mobile game published by **JMUnlimited**. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Perfect Stop mobile application.

## Information We Collect

Perfect Stop does **not collect, transmit, store on external servers, sell, or share personal information**.

The app does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as a name, email address, phone number, or location.

## Local App Data

Perfect Stop may store game-related information locally on your device, such as:

- Game settings
- High scores
- Gameplay statistics
- Preferences such as sound settings

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to JMUnlimited or any external server.

Removing the app or clearing its application data may permanently remove this locally stored information.

## Internet Access

Perfect Stop does not require an internet connection for its core gameplay and does not use a JMUnlimited server to transmit gameplay or personal information.

## Advertising

Perfect Stop does not contain third-party advertising.

## Analytics and Tracking

Perfect Stop does not use third-party analytics, behavioral tracking, or advertising-tracking technologies.

## Third-Party Services

Perfect Stop does not intentionally transmit personal information to third-party services as part of its gameplay.

If future versions introduce services that affect how user information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

## Children's Privacy

Perfect Stop does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information, JMUnlimited does not maintain personal information about children through Perfect Stop.

## Data Security

Perfect Stop is designed to minimize privacy risks by keeping game information locally on the user's device and avoiding unnecessary collection or transmission of user information.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

JMUnlimited may update this Privacy Policy if Perfect Stop's features or data practices change.

Changes will be reflected on this page by updating the "Last Updated" date.
